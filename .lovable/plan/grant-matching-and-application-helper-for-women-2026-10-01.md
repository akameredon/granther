# Grant matching and application helper for women

## Name
Suggested name: **GrantHer**. It's short, easy to remember, and says who it's for. Other options:
- **Fundwise**: smart, calm, and trustworthy
- **HerFund**: warm and direct
- **Qualifi**: focuses on the "do I qualify?" filter

The app will use GrantHer unless you pick another name.

## What users get
1. **Sign up and profile**: She enters her country, age, business stage, industry, revenue, and a short story about her business. This only needs to be done once.
2. **Grant matches (the filter)**: Every grant is marked as Eligible, Maybe, or Not for you, with a plain-language reason for each.
3. **Grant explainer**: Each grant gets one page that covers:
   - what it is
   - who funds it, what they look for, and the kind of winners they usually pick
   - the requirements
   - a checklist of things to prepare before applying
4. **Application writer**: She picks a question from the grant (for example, "Personal statement, max 500 words") and gets a ready-to-paste answer based on her profile. She can copy or regenerate it.
5. **Subscription**: Matches are free to browse. The explainer and writer need a monthly plan.

## What you (admin) get
- An admin page to add and edit grants: title, funder, amount, deadline, link, eligibility rules, required questions, and funder background notes.

## Look
A warm, confident look in deep green and gold on cream, with a classic serif for headings. Clean and calm, with no clutter.

## Technical details
- Lovable Cloud for accounts, data (profiles, grants, saved drafts), and an admin role kept in its own roles table.
- Lovable AI for checking eligibility, writing the explainers, and drafting applications. All AI runs on the server.
- Payments are added after the core works. I'll recommend a provider and confirm the monthly price with you then.
- Pages: home, sign in, profile, grants list, grant details, writer, admin.
